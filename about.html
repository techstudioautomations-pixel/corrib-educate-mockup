About Corrib Educate

An Irish welcome. Learn through exploring Ireland.

For more than three decades, we have helped international schools experience Ireland with curiosity, confidence and care.

Our story

How it all started.

Corrib Educate has welcomed international school groups to Ireland since 1989—starting out in B&B stays and growing into a trusted name in educational travel. More than 35 years on, we're still doing what we do best: matching schools with the right hosts, places and experiences.

What guides us

Our goal.

Our aim is to ensure every student gets the best experience possible in our beautiful country, ensuring the best of host accommodation and services throughout your unique stay.

Our team

The people behind every stay.

Padraig Ryan

Director

After completing an executive MBA at the University of Galway in 2018 and 17 years in management roles across the pharmaceutical industry, Padraig moved into the education sector in 2019. Since then, Corrib Educate has given the group a strong foothold in the education and operations space, and Padraig works across the team to guide strategic direction.

Maria Fernanda Arredondo

Finance & Invoicing

Originally from Mexico, Fernanda moved to Ireland to study Strategic Management at the University of Galway. A fluent Spanish and French speaker, she brings strong experience in finance and invoicing, and is approachable and solution-orientated.

Sharon Cooley

Administration

Sharon brings a wealth of experience in operations management at medical device level, plus childcare management. Organised and hands-on, she deals directly with host families and third-party suppliers, and has hosted Spanish students herself, so knows first-hand the role host families play for their students.

Maela Ni Choistealbha

Academic Programmes

Maela looks after the academic programmes for students. A qualified English teacher and former Head of Studies with Move Language Ahead, she also speaks French, and guides and directs our English language offering for mini-stays and summer language programmes.
